Detailed Description

template<typename T, unsigned Scale>
requires (Scale <= max_scale)
struct snicholls::with_scale< T, Scale >

An asset type with a scale given by its user: with_scale<currency::XAU, 18> is gold at 18 places.

ISO 4217 gives the precious metals, the SDR and the funds no minor units, and SIX's historic list gives none for withdrawn codes, so their types have no scale() and are not asset_like until one is chosen.
