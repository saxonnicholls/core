Detailed Description

template<std::size_t Bytes>
struct snicholls::digest_hex< Bytes >

A digest's hexadecimal text: 2 * Bytes lowercase digits and a '\0', held by value.

There is no implicit conversion to std::string_view: std::string_view v = d.hex(); would view a temporary that is gone at the semicolon. Keep the text (auto text = d.hex();) and take text.view().
