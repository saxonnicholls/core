Detailed Description

Internal: the signed webhooks of core's in-memory reference providers (memory_payment_provider, memory_security_provider), written once.

A Memory-Signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex> header, the tag HMAC-SHA256 under the webhook secret over <t>.<raw body> (Stripe's scheme), several v1 accepted during a secret rotation, a 300-second window; the body key=value lines of a webhook_event's fields, its kind by the enumerator's name. Not public API; tested in tests/detail/memory_webhook_test.cpp and through both references' kits.
