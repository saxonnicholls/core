Detailed Description

snicholls::finance::cash_flow: one dated cash flow, exact, positive in and negative out.

Overview. The one type for money that moves on a day, shared by everything in finance/ that reads dated flows: the liquidity ladder (make_cash_ladder, cash_ladder.hpp), which dates flows by their value date, and the return measures (modified_dietz, money_weighted_return, returns.hpp), which date external flows by the day they happened. One convention everywhere: a flow in (received, contributed, a settlement credit) is positive, a flow out is negative. The amount is exact money; xirr_flow in returns.hpp is the double form for the root finder.

// a settlement credit of 1,250.00 due on 4 March, and a payout of 300.00 on the 5th: positive in, negative out
const finance::cash_flow due[] = {{std::chrono::sys_days{2026y / 3 / 4}, money::from_units(aud, 125'000)},
{std::chrono::sys_days{2026y / 3 / 5}, money::from_units(aud, -30'000)}};
// the same span goes to finance::make_cash_ladder (liquidity) or finance::modified_dietz (performance)
