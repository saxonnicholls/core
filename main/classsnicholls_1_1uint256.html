◆ parse_units()

constexpr std::optional< uint256 > snicholls::uint256::parse_units ( std::string_view text,
unsigned decimals )
inlinestaticnodiscardconstexprnoexcept

The base units in a decimal amount of a token with decimals decimals: ethers' parseUnits.

parse_units("10000.5", 6) is 10,000,500,000 (USDT and USDC have 6 decimals; ETH 18). Digits beyond decimals are accepted only when they are zeros, so Binance's eight-place strings ("0.00100000") parse for a 6-decimal token, but nothing is ever rounded away.

Parameters
textDigits with at most one .: "12", "12.5", ".5", "12."; no sign, no separators.
decimalsThe token's decimals, at most 77.
Returns
The base units, or std::nullopt for malformed text, a non-zero digit past decimals, or overflow.
