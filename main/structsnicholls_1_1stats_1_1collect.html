Detailed Description

template<typename T>
struct snicholls::stats::collect< T >

Every value, kept: the cell of a pivot partitioned rather than aggregated (a raw pivot table), so that any statistic, a median, a drawdown or a full bootstrap, can be computed per cell afterwards.
