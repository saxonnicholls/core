Detailed Description

ggml's NVFP4: 64 FP4 (E2M1) numbers in four groups of 16, each with an unsigned E4M3 scale.

ggml's form of NVIDIA's NVFP4 has no per-tensor FP32 scale: a tensor's scale is applied outside the block. Group g covers elements 16g to 16g + 15; within it, element j is the low nibble of byte 8g + j and j + 8 the high, so each group is packed as a Q4_0 block of 16.
