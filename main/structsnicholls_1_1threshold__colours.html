Detailed Description

Colours by ascending cut points: threshold_colours{colours::green, {{5, colours::yellow}, {15, colours::red}}} is green below 5, yellow from 5, red from 15 (staleness in seconds, a book's age).

Wins over sign_colours and colour for a number. Up to eight cut points, checked ascending by the compiler.
