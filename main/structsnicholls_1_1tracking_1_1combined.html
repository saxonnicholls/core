Detailed Description

template<transition_model M, transition_model... Ms>
struct snicholls::tracking::combined< M, Ms >

Several models side by side, each over its own block of the state: block-diagonal F and Q (Stone Soup's CombinedLinearGaussianTransitionModel and CombinedGaussianTransitionModel).
