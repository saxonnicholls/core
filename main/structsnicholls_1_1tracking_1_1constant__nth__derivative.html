template<linalg::scalar T, std::size_t N>
struct snicholls::tracking::constant_nth_derivative< T, N >

A value and its first N derivatives, the N-th constant but for white noise of diffusion q.

Template Parameters
TThe number type.
NThe derivative held constant: 0 random walk, 1 constant velocity, 2 constant acceleration.
