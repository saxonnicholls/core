ml Directory Reference

Files

 		activations.hpp
 snicholls::ml activations: sigmoid, softplus, logit, ReLU, ReLU², SiLU, GELU (exact and tanh) and the logit soft cap, each with its derivative, all constexpr.
 		attention.hpp
 snicholls::ml attention for one query: attend over a block of keys and values in one pass, its backward pass, attention_accumulator, the partial that merges across workers, attention_window for causal and sliding-window masks, and attention_keys for every attention_mask: causal, bidirectional and block-causal, with key padding.
 		attention_layer.hpp
 snicholls::ml::attention_forward, attention_forward_sequence and attention_backward: a transformer's attention for one token at one position, or over a sequence for training, and its backward pass: the Q, K and V projections, QK norm, RoPE, the KV cache, grouped-query attention, the soft cap and the output projection, as the configuration says.
 		attention_sink.hpp
 snicholls::ml::attend_with_sink and its backward pass: attention for one query with a learnable sink logit, a virtual key every query attends to that carries no value (gpt-oss), with keys and values of different widths.
 		bpe.hpp
 snicholls::ml::bpe_encode_piece, bpe_encode_long_piece and bpe_decode: byte-level BPE, token for token what tiktoken gives, with the vocabulary as the caller's lookup and no allocation.
 		collapse.hpp
 snicholls::ml collapse safeguards for joint-embedding training: embedding_moments (mergeable per-dimension standard deviation, covariance and effective rank), VICReg's variance and covariance terms, and LeJEPA's SIGReg (sketched Epps-Pulley), each with its gradient.
 		composed_block.hpp
 snicholls::ml transformer blocks composed from an attention part and an MLP part, either kind of each: x += attention(norm(x)); x += mlp(norm(x)), batched for training (returning the MLP's auxiliary losses) and token by token for decoding; DeepSeek-V3's block, multi-head latent attention with a mixture of experts, as latent_moe_block_* with its buffers' sizes and layouts.
 		diloco.hpp
 snicholls::ml arithmetic of data-parallel training across machines: the fixed-order sum of the workers' parts, DiLoCo's pseudo-gradient, its bfloat16 compression with error feedback, and its outer step.
 		distill.hpp
 snicholls::ml knowledge distillation: the forward and reverse KL between a teacher's and a student's distributions at a temperature, fused and stable, with the student's gradient; and the variant that keeps only the teacher's top-k logits.
 		embedding.hpp
 snicholls::ml embeddings: a token's row of a table, GPT-2's token plus position, and the backward pass that adds a gradient into the token's row.
 		expert_parallel.hpp
 snicholls::ml expert parallelism, the arithmetic: which rank holds which experts, the dispatch and combine layouts, and a reference all-to-all over in-process ranks that fixes the order of every received row.
 		gather.hpp
 snicholls::ml rows by index lists, on the backend: gather_rows, its backward scatter_add_rows, scatter_rows, concat_rows and slice_rows, over a batch of sequences, deterministic.
 		jepa.hpp
 snicholls::ml joint embedding predictive architectures: the predictor's configuration, sizes, input and output, the normalised targets, the prediction losses (L2, L1, smooth L1, cosine) with their gradients, and the target encoder as an exponential moving average with its momentum schedule.
 		kv_cache.hpp
 snicholls::ml::kv_cache_view and kv_cache: the keys and values of every layer and key-value head, laid out so one head's keys are contiguous, with a sliding window as a ring.
 		latent_attention.hpp
 snicholls::ml multi-head latent attention (MLA, DeepSeek-V2 and V3): queries, keys and values from low-rank latents with decoupled RoPE; the training passes over sequences through a linalg backend; a KV cache holding only the latent and the rotary key; and the absorbed form for decoding.
 		latent_block.hpp
 snicholls::ml transformer blocks whose attention is multi-head latent attention: x += mla(norm(x)); x += mlp(norm(x)), for decoding with a latent cache and, for training, over a batch of sequences with its backward pass.
 		linear.hpp
 snicholls::ml::linear: y = W x + b over any weight storage: dense rows of float, double or bfloat16, or ggml's quantised blocks.
 		lm_head.hpp
 snicholls::ml::lm_head_loss: a language model's loss over a sequence, the head's logits and the cross-entropy fused, and its backward pass into the hidden states and the head (tied or not).
 		logprobs.hpp
 snicholls::ml log-probabilities of given tokens: per token from logits (fused and stable), with the entropy, summed or averaged over a sequence's response tokens, and their backward passes.
 		lora.hpp
 snicholls::ml LoRA: a trainable low-rank update y = W x + (α/r) B A x beside a frozen base (dense, bfloat16 or NF4 for QLoRA), forward and backward for one row, a sequence and attention's heads, its initialisation, merge and unmerge, and the optimiser's view of the adapter alone.
 		masking.hpp
 snicholls::ml masks for joint embedding predictive architectures: I-JEPA's multi-block sampler, V-JEPA's spatio-temporal tubes, random masks, and the collation of index lists across a batch, deterministic from (seed, step, sample).
 		minifloat.hpp
 snicholls::ml::minifloat<E, M, Kind>: the low-precision floating-point formats of machine learning (bfloat16, float16, FP8 E4M3 and E5M2, the MX FP6 and FP4) as one template, with exact decoding, round-to-nearest-even and stochastic rounding, all constexpr.
 		mlp.hpp
 snicholls::ml::mlp_forward and mlp_backward: a transformer's MLP for one token, down(act(up x)) or SwiGLU's down(silu(gate x) · up x), as the configuration says, and its backward pass; moe_gate and its backward.
 		moe.hpp
 snicholls::ml mixture of experts for training: routing with a selection bias, the expert permutation, gather and combine, the grouped products and the experts' MLPs, the balance losses and the bias rule, load statistics, and the layer moe_forward / moe_backward over a batch of rows.
 		moe_block.hpp
 snicholls::ml::block_forward_sequence and block_backward for a block whose MLP is a mixture of experts: x += attention(norm(x)); x += moe(norm(x)), batched through a backend.
 		multi_token_prediction.hpp
 snicholls::ml multi-token prediction (DeepSeek-V3): a chain of modules, each predicting one token further ahead from the previous depth's hidden states and the next token's embedding through one transformer block and the model's own output head; forward and backward passes over a batch of sequences, and the weighted losses.
 		muon.hpp
 snicholls::ml::muon_step: the Muon optimiser for a weight matrix, Newton-Schulz orthogonalisation of its momentum through a linalg backend.
 		nf4.hpp
 snicholls::ml QLoRA's 4-bit NormalFloat weights with double quantisation: quantise, dequantise, and products that dequantise on the fly.
 		norm.hpp
 snicholls::ml normalisation: layer_norm and rms_norm over one row, forward and backward, with or without learned parameters.
 		optim.hpp
 snicholls::ml training arithmetic: the AdamW step, learning-rate schedules, clipping by the global norm, Muon's Newton-Schulz polynomial, and the FLOPs and utilisation of a transformer.
 		patch.hpp
 snicholls::ml patches and positions for vision transformers: patch_grid, patchify_rows / unpatchify_rows for images, video tubelets and spectrograms, fixed sine-cosine position embeddings in two and three dimensions, axial RoPE, and gathered_rope for tokens at scattered positions.
 		preference.hpp
 snicholls::ml preference losses over (chosen, rejected) pairs: DPO, IPO, SimPO and ORPO, each with its gradient in the policy's sequence log-probabilities, and their metrics as a mergeable accumulator.
 		probe.hpp
 snicholls::ml probes of frozen features: mean_pool_rows and the attentive probe (one learned query, one cross-attention, as V-JEPA's AttentivePooler), forward and backward.
 		quantize.hpp
 snicholls::ml quantised weights: ggml's Q8_0 and Q4_0 blocks and the OCP MXFP4 block, byte for byte as ggml lays them out, with quantisation, dequantisation and the integer dot products, plus symmetric int8 rows.
 		reward_model.hpp
 snicholls::ml reward models: a scalar value head on hidden states, the Bradley-Terry pairwise loss on its rewards, and each sequence's last response position.
 		rl.hpp
 snicholls::ml reinforcement learning for language models: GRPO's group-normalised advantages, the clipped importance-ratio objective with the k3 KL penalty and an entropy bonus, the normalisations of GRPO, DAPO and Dr. GRPO, and PPO's GAE and value loss.
 		rope.hpp
 snicholls::ml rotary position embedding: the frequencies, a table of cosines and sines the compiler can build, and the rotation of a head, forward and inverse.
 		row.hpp
 snicholls::ml::row and mutable_row: what the ml functions accept as a row of numbers, and the spans they work on.
 		sampling.hpp
 snicholls::ml sampling the next token: argmax, sampling from probabilities or from logits at a temperature, top_k and top-k sampling with a fixed-size buffer, and nucleus (top-p) sampling with the caller's scratch.
 		self_attention.hpp
 snicholls::ml::self_attention_head and its backward pass: one self-attention head over a sequence (causal, bidirectional or block-causal), from its own Q, K and V projections, with no configuration and no cache.
 		softmax.hpp
 snicholls::ml softmax and losses: softmax, log_softmax, logsumexp, cross-entropy with its fused gradient, bits per byte, and online_softmax, a running softmax that merges across workers.
 		sparse_attention.hpp
 snicholls::ml::attend_selected and its backward pass, attention over the keys an index list selects; and DeepSeek Sparse Attention's lightning indexer: its scores, their backward pass, the top-k selection and the indexer's KL loss.
 		transformer_block.hpp
 snicholls::ml::block_forward: one pre-norm transformer block, x += attention(norm(x)); x += mlp(norm(x)), for a token or a run of tokens; and for training, over a sequence with its backward pass.
 		transformer_config.hpp
 snicholls::ml::transformer_config: the architecture of a transformer layer as one compile-time value, from which every size is a constant.
