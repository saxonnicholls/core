Detailed Description

template<std::size_t P, point W>
struct snicholls::metric::weighted_minkowski< P, W >

The weighted Minkowski distance (Σ wᵢ |aᵢ - bᵢ|ᴾ)^(1/P), with one weight per axis (a point of weights).

Template Parameters
PThe order, at least 1.
WThe weights' type, a point (fixed_vector, std::array, a span over storage that outlives the metric).
