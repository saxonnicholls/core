Detailed Description

snicholls::ml preference losses over (chosen, rejected) pairs: DPO, IPO, SimPO and ORPO, each with its gradient in the policy's sequence log-probabilities, and their metrics as a mergeable accumulator.

Overview. Preference optimisation trains a policy on pairs of responses to one prompt, one preferred (chosen, y_w) and one not (rejected, y_l), without a reward model or sampling (TRAINING.md T13). Each loss is a function of the policy's sequence log-probabilities of the two responses (ml/logprobs.hpp) and, for some, a frozen reference model's; its gradient in those two numbers is taken back to the logits by sequence_logprobs_backward and token_logprobs_rows_backward. The losses, each a parameter struct and an overload of preference_loss(params, chosen, rejected, ref_chosen, ref_rejected):

Loss Paper Loss of one pair Log-probabilities
dpo{beta} Rafailov et al. 2023, eq. 7 -log σ(β[(π_w - ref_w) - (π_l - ref_l)]) sums
ipo{tau} Azar et al. 2023, eq. 17 (h - 1/(2τ))², h = (π_w - ref_w) - (π_l - ref_l) sums (the caller's choice)
simpo{beta, gamma} Meng et al. 2024, eq. 6 -log σ(β π̄_w - β π̄_l - γ) means; no reference
orpo{lambda} Hong et al. 2024, eqs. 3-7 -π̄_w + λ · -log σ(log odds(π̄_w) - log odds(π̄_l)) means; no reference

Here π_w is log π_θ(y_w | x), ref_w the reference's, and π̄ a mean over the response's tokens (logprob_reduction::mean). ORPO's odds(p̄) = P / (1 - P) with P = e^π̄ (its eq. 3 and appendix A: the geometric mean of the tokens' probabilities), so log odds = π̄ - log(-expm1(π̄)); its SFT term is the chosen response's mean negative log-likelihood, -π̄_w. Each loss is computed through softplus, so it never takes log(0).

Every overload returns a pair_loss: the loss, its derivatives in the policy's two log-probabilities, and the implicit rewards the training logs report (DPO: β (π - ref), its section 5; IPO: τ (π - ref), as TRL reports it; SimPO: β π̄; ORPO: λ π̄). preference_metrics adds pairs and merges across workers: the mean loss, the rewards, the margin and the accuracy (the fraction of pairs whose chosen reward is strictly the larger).

// Sequence log-probabilities (sums over the response) of the policy and of the frozen reference
const double chosen = -12.0, rejected = -15.0, ref_chosen = -13.0, ref_rejected = -14.0;
const ml::pair_loss<double> d = ml::preference_loss(ml::dpo{.beta = 0.1}, chosen, rejected, ref_chosen, ref_rejected);
// d.loss = -log sigma(0.1 ((-12 + 13) - (-15 + 14))) = -log sigma(0.2); d.d_chosen = -0.1 sigma(-0.2)

preference_loss_rows(backend, params, ...) is the batch on the backend (TRAINING.md T9): every pair's loss, the gradients of the mean written per pair, the metrics added.

// A batch of three pairs: the losses, the gradients of the mean, and the metrics, on the backend
const std::array<double, 3> pc{-10, -20, -8}, pr{-12, -18, -9}, rc{-11, -19, -8.5}, rr{-11, -19, -8.5};
std::array<double, 3> dc{}, dr{};
ml::preference_metrics<double> worker0, worker1;
const double mean_loss = ml::preference_loss_rows<double>(backend, ml::dpo{.beta = 0.5}, pc, pr, rc, rr, dc, dr, worker0);
worker1.merge(worker0); // metrics from many workers merge

Relationships. ml/logprobs.hpp (the sequence log-probabilities and their backward), ml/activations.hpp (softplus, sigmoid), ml/reward_model.hpp (the Bradley-Terry loss, an overload of preference_loss in this shape). Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations. One each. A loss of the caller's own joins by overloading preference_loss for its parameter struct in its own namespace; preference_loss_rows finds it by argument-dependent lookup.

Guarantees. constexpr, no allocation, noexcept for the standard floating-point types; O(1) a pair. The parameter structs, pair_loss and preference_metrics are trivially copyable; merge is commutative and associative up to rounding of the sums.

Errors. Preconditions, asserted: the spans' sizes agree; ORPO's mean log-probabilities are below 0 (a response of probability 1 has infinite odds). Identical chosen and rejected give DPO log 2 with gradients ∓β/2, IPO 1/(4τ²).

Definitions. DPO, IPO, SimPO, ORPO and their gradients: checked against the papers (arXiv 2305.18290 eq. 7 and the gradient after it; 2310.12036 eq. 17; 2405.14734 eq. 6; 2403.07691 eqs. 3-10). The reported rewards of IPO and ORPO follow TRL's logging, recalled, not from the papers. Default parameters: DPO's β = 0.1 (the paper's smallest), SimPO's β = 2, γ = 1 (within its recommended β ∈ [2, 2.5], γ ∈ [0.5, 1.5]), ORPO's λ = 0.1 (its Mistral runs); IPO's τ = 0.1 is a common choice, recalled.

Performance. O(1) a pair; the batch is negligible next to the log-probabilities it consumes. Not benchmarked.

Tests and demos. tests/ml/preference_test.cpp (each loss against a direct computation, central differences for every gradient, the edge cases, a tiny DPO run whose margin rises), demos/ml/preference_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <cassert>, <cstddef>, <cstdint>, <span>, <type_traits>, snicholls/core/math/constexpr_math.hpp, ml/activations.hpp.

Known issues. DPO's label smoothing (conservative DPO), KTO and the other variants are not here; each would be one more overload.

Status. Experimental, new in 0.16.0.
