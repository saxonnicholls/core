Detailed Description

snicholls::ml multi-token prediction (DeepSeek-V3): a chain of modules, each predicting one token further ahead from the previous depth's hidden states and the next token's embedding through one transformer block and the model's own output head; forward and backward passes over a batch of sequences, and the weighted losses.

Overview. DeepSeek-V3 (DeepSeek-AI, 2024, section 2.2) trains each position to predict D tokens beyond the next, sequentially, so the causal chain is kept at every depth. Module k (1 to D), at position i: 

h'^k_i = M_k [RMSNorm(h^{k-1}_i); RMSNorm(Emb(t_{i+k}))]        (eq. 21; h^0 is the main model's output)
h^k = TRM_k(h'^k)                                              (eq. 22, one transformer block)
P^k_{i+k+1} = OutHead(h^k_i)                                   (eq. 23, the main model's head, shared)
L^k = -(1/T) Σ_{i=2+k}^{T+1} log P^k_i[t_i],   L_MTP = (λ/D) Σ_k L^k   (eqs. 24, 25)

M_k is d x 2d, its columns the previous depth's half then the embedding's; the embedding table and the head are the main model's. Every depth here keeps the sequence's length T (as large-scale implementations do, so the shapes never change): position i reads the embedding of token i + k, and the last k positions, which have no such token, read a padding id (mtp_input_ids) and are left out of the loss, so they influence nothing that is scored (each position sees only earlier ones). The loss of depth k divides by T, as eq. 24 does, not by the T - k positions it scores.

  • mtp_combine_rows and mtp_combine_rows_backward: the two RMS norms and the concatenation, on the backend (TRAINING.md T9).
  • mtp_forward_sequence(backend, w, hidden_prev, embedded, rope_src, act, sequences): the combine, M_k, the block (any block with batched passes: block_weights, latent_block_weights, moe_block_weights, composed_block_weights such as DeepSeek-V3's latent_moe_block_weights), and the norm before the shared head; writes act.output (h^k, the next depth's hidden_prev) and act.head_input, and returns the block's auxiliary losses when it has a mixture of experts (V3's modules use the main model's block kind).
  • mtp_backward(...): from the gradient in the head's input (and in h^k from the depth after), the gradients in h^{k-1} and in the embedded rows (added into; embed_rows_backward takes them to the shared table), and every weight's.
  • mtp_loss_sequence(backend, head, head_input, targets, depth, ...): lm_head_loss_sequence over the scored positions of each sequence with weight λ/D and denominator sequences · T, its gradients added into.

At inference the modules can be dropped: the main model does not read them (V3 section 2.2). Or they draft: module 1 on the main model's last hidden state and the token just sampled proposes the token after it, which the main model verifies in its next step (speculative decoding; V3 reports 85 to 90% acceptance of the second token, section 5.4.3). Decoding a module one token at a time is its block's block_forward on h'^k, with a cache of its own.

Relationships. ml/lm_head.hpp (the shared head), ml/embedding.hpp (embed_rows, embed_rows_backward), ml/transformer_block.hpp, ml/latent_block.hpp, ml/moe_block.hpp and ml/composed_block.hpp (the blocks), ml/norm.hpp, transformer_config::mtp_depth and mtp_loss_weight. Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations. One, generic over the block.

Guarantees. constexpr, no allocation. A module costs its block plus a d x 2d product per position. The gradients are added into, so depths and micro-batches accumulate.

Errors. Preconditions, asserted: rows of d_model; depth below T; the projection d x 2d; working spaces of their documented sizes.

Performance. Not benchmarked on its own: a block, one product and the head's loss, each benchmarked where it is defined.

Tests and demos. tests/ml/multi_token_prediction_test.cpp, demos/ml/multi_token_prediction_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <array>, <cassert>, <cstddef>, <cstdint>, <span>, <type_traits>, linalg/backend.hpp, ml/linear.hpp, ml/lm_head.hpp, ml/norm.hpp, ml/transformer_block.hpp, ml/transformer_config.hpp.

Known issues. The block's overloads are found by argument-dependent lookup: include its header (ml/latent_block.hpp for a latent-attention block, ml/composed_block.hpp for DeepSeek-V3's). With a mixture-of-experts block the last k positions, padded and unscored, are still routed: they count in the balance loss and the z-loss and so reach the router's gradient (the loss's other terms ignore them). Released DeepSeek-V3 checkpoints store M_k with the embedding's columns first (eh_proj over [enorm(e); hnorm(h)], recalled from the open implementations): swap the halves when loading.

Status. Experimental, new in 0.16.0.

One multi-token prediction module over a batch of sequences: combine, project, the block, the head's norm.

The module returns what its block's block_forward_sequence returns: nothing for a dense block, the auxiliary losses (moe_losses) for a block with a mixture of experts (ml/moe_block.hpp, ml/composed_block.hpp), as DeepSeek-V3's modules have. Those losses are the block's like any other block's, already weighted by the configuration's balance_loss_weight and z_loss_weight and not by λ / D: the caller adds them to the total as it adds the main model's blocks', and mtp_backward includes their gradients.
