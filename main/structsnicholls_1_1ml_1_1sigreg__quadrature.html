Detailed Description

template<math::math_number T, std::size_t K = 17>
requires (K >= 3 && K % 2 == 1)
struct snicholls::ml::sigreg_quadrature< T, K >

SIGReg's quadrature: K knots t_k = k · t_max / (K - 1) on [0, t_max], the normal's characteristic function φ_k = e^(-t_k²/2) there, and the weights w_k = φ_k · 2 Δt (Δt at the two ends), the trapezoid rule on [-t_max, t_max] by symmetry times the Gaussian window.
