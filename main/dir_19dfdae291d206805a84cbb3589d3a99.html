 		discrete.hpp
 snicholls::tracking::hmm_predict, hmm_update and hmm_predict_measurement: the Bayes filter of a hidden Markov model, for a target's class or mode.
 		fusion.hpp
 snicholls::tracking::covariance_intersection, optimal_omega and fast_ci_weights: fusing estimates whose cross-correlation is unknown, without becoming overconfident.
 		gaussian.hpp
 snicholls::tracking::gaussian<T, N> and the measures between states: Mahalanobis distance, log-likelihood, Hellinger distance, KL divergence, the χ² gate and the confidence ellipse.
 		kalman.hpp
 snicholls::tracking::predict, update, predict_measurement and friends: the Kalman and extended Kalman filter steps, the information form, the Rauch-Tung-Striebel smoothing step, the alpha-beta filter and Fisher information.
 		measurement.hpp
 snicholls::tracking::linear_gaussian, bearing_range, elevation_bearing_range and the other sensor models: what a sensor measures of a state, its Jacobian, its noise, and which components are angles.
 		mixture.hpp
 snicholls::tracking::reduce_to_single, merge_pair, runnalls_cost and reduce_mixture: Gaussian mixtures kept small enough to carry forward.
 		transition.hpp
 snicholls::tracking::constant_velocity, singer, constant_turn, combined and the other motion models: how a state moves over a time step, and how uncertain the move is.
 		unscented.hpp
