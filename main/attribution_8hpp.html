Detailed Description

P&L explained by greeks and factors, and risk by contributor: Taylor terms, shared fairly by Shapley.

Overview. A position's P&L over a move of its risk factors is, to second order, sum g_i dx_i + 1/2 sum H_ij dx_i dx_j + carry. Each term is a greek's P&L: delta, gamma, vega, volga, theta, rho, and the cross terms such as vanna (spot and volatility together). explain reports every term, the part the model leaves unexplained against the actual P&L, and the P&L by factor: each factor's own terms plus half of every cross term it is in. That split is the Shapley attribution of the Taylor model, exactly: its pair dividends are the cross terms, and Shapley shares each equally between the pair. So "how much was spot, how much was volatility" has the one fair answer, without the 2^n revaluations of game::attribute_change.

// A book of two positions in one underlying: greeks add, scaled by quantity.
constexpr finance::greeks<> call{.delta = 0.55, .gamma = 0.04, .vega = 0.20, .vanna = 0.01, .volga = 0.30, .theta = -0.02, .rho = 0.25};
constexpr finance::greeks<> put{.delta = -0.45, .gamma = 0.04, .vega = 0.20, .vanna = 0.01, .volga = 0.30, .theta = -0.015, .rho = -0.24};
constexpr auto straddle = call * 100.0 + put * 100.0;
// Spot up 2, implied volatility up 1.5 points, one day, rates flat; the P&L from full revaluation.
constexpr finance::market_move<> today{.spot = 2.0, .vol = 1.5, .time = 1.0, .rate = 0.0};
constexpr auto why = finance::explain(straddle, today, 162.4);

For one underlying, greeks and market_move name the terms; for a book of several underlyings with cross-gammas, taylor_model<K> is the general form. Greeks add across positions (+, scaled by *), so a book's explain is its positions' summed. "Long or short volatility" is the sign of vega, and the volatility P&L is vega + volga + vanna / 2.

Risk is explained the same way. risk_contributions splits a portfolio's variance into each holding's w_i (Sigma w)_i, which is both the Euler allocation and the Shapley attribution of the variance game; the volatility split it reports, the same shares divided by sigma, is Euler's, and is not the Shapley attribution of volatility, which game::shapley computes when wanted.

Relationships. game/shapley.hpp, game/harsanyi.hpp, game/change.hpp (exact by revaluation). Group: snicholls-core.

Guarantees. constexpr, noexcept, no allocation. explained is the sum of the terms; the factors' P&L sums to explained - carry.

Errors. None.

Performance. O(K^2); not benchmarked.

Tests and demos. tests/finance/attribution_test.cpp, demos/finance/attribution_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <array>, <cmath>, <concepts>, <cstddef>.

Known issues. The greeks are in the units of the moves: a vega per unit of volatility with the move in units of volatility, a theta per year with the move in years. Third-order terms (speed, ultima, zomma) are not modelled and land in unexplained.

Status. New in 0.5.0, experimental.
