Detailed Description

snicholls::payment_provider: anything that moves fiat money in and out (collections, payouts, refunds), verifies its webhooks and gives a ledger authoritative facts.

Overview. Three movements cover every provider:

Movement A pull provider (Stripe) A push provider (Wise)
collection, money in from a payer a checkout session or payment intent: we pull, the payer confirms an inbound bank transfer: the payer pushes to our account details with a reference, and a webhook says it arrived
payout, money out to a beneficiary a payout to our bank a transfer to a registered recipient, optionally on a quote
refund, money back to a payer a refund of a charge unsupported (a payout to the payer instead)

collect returns the payment and the payer's next_step: a redirect (a hosted checkout), a client confirmation (a secret for the browser SDK), or bank details with the reference to quote. Status then arrives by webhook (verify_webhook, which authenticates and parses without I/O) or by asking (status). A push provider's credits are listed by credits for reconciliation. This is the concept half of a pair; the pure virtual half is payment_provider_interface (interfaces/payment_provider.hpp), with payment_provider_adapter<P> and payment_provider_ref.

The contract (interfaces/payment_provider_conformance.hpp checks it):

  • Every amount is an exact money (money/money.hpp), never a double or an int64. A collection, payout or refund amount is positive; zero or negative is invalid_argument.
  • Every request that moves money carries an idempotency_key (at most 64 characters, client_id; so is our_reference). The same key with the same request returns the same result and moves money once; with a different request it is conflict.
  • What a provider does not do (a method, a currency, payouts, quotes, refunds, credits) is unsupported, and capabilities() says so in advance.
  • verify_webhook fails closed: no signature, or a wrong one, is signature_invalid; a delivery outside the provider's time window is stale. It never contacts the network and is safe on any input. This holds in every mode, tests included: there is no unsigned path. The caller deduplicates deliveries on dedup_key() and books money once per fact_id.
  • An event that names a payment but carries no detail has needs_lookup set: the caller asks status or credits.
  • A refund never exceeds what was received less what was refunded (conflict otherwise).
  • next_step::client_secret and every credential are secrets: they print as ********.
  • The webhook flow. The route calls verify_webhook first; then inserts dedup_key() with the raw event atomically and answers 2xx; then a durable worker calls resolve, which fetches the authoritative data (the provider's balance transaction or statement line) and returns one payment_fact per line. Amounts never come from the webhook body, which is why webhook_event carries none. An event that books nothing resolves to no facts.
  • Stripe's events to facts: payment_intent.succeeded, checkout.session.completed and async_payment_succeeded to payment (one txn_); charge.refunded to refund; refund.updated (failed) to refund_failed; charge.dispute.created and funds_withdrawn to dispute_hold; funds_reinstated or closed (won) to dispute_release; closed (lost) to dispute_loss; payout.paid to payout; payout.failed to payout_failed. Every other event is recorded and produces no fact.
  • statement reads the provider's lines for the reconcilers. Every fact passes validate: gross == net + fee. Every statement line maps to exactly one fact, with fact_id the provider's line id: lines that are not payments are fee, conversion, transfer, adjustment (reviewed, never auto-posted), card_spend or other (reviewed), never skipped. Facts are oldest first within a page; the reconciler matches by fact_id as a set.
  • dispute_loss books no new money movement: its fact_id is <withdrawal txn>:dispute_loss, gross the disputed amount as withdrawn, fee zero, net == gross.
  • The map from provider account to book is the product's; a provider knows only the opaque account id.
  • Errors follow the redaction rule of capability_error.hpp: a decline's provider_code is the provider's code (card_declined), never its message, and never card or payer data.
auto provider = make_provider();
const auto c = checkout(provider, "order-1", *parse_money(aud, "49.95"));
// the payer follows c->next.url; the provider tells us by webhook, and resolve gives the ledger its fact
static_cast<void>(provider.complete(c->pay.payment_id.view()));
webhook_event e;
e.source = "memory";
e.event_id = "evt_1";
e.kind = event_kind::payment_updated;
e.payment_id = c->pay.payment_id;
const auto facts = provider.resolve(e);

Relationships. money/money.hpp, money/rate.hpp, http_header.hpp, secret.hpp, uuid.hpp, capability_error.hpp, capability_ids.hpp, time/utc.hpp; the twin interfaces/payment_provider.hpp, the reference interfaces/payment_provider_reference.hpp, the kit interfaces/payment_provider_conformance.hpp. Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations. memory_payment_provider (interfaces/payment_provider_reference.hpp, both shapes, for tests). Above core: Stripe and Wise.

Guarantees. is_terminal, balances and validate are constexpr and noexcept; dedup_key is allocation-free. Thread safety is the implementation's.

Errors. capability_error, as the contract says.

Performance. Webhook verification is the hot path: benchmarks/interfaces/payment_provider_reference_benchmark.cpp.

Tests and demos. tests/concepts/payment_provider_test.cpp, demos/concepts/payment_provider_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <array>, <concepts>, <cstddef>, <cstdint>, <optional>, <span>, <string>, <string_view>, <utility>, <vector>, capability_error.hpp, capability_ids.hpp, http_header.hpp, money/money.hpp, money/rate.hpp, secret.hpp, time/utc.hpp, uuid.hpp.

Known issues. fx_quote holds a rate, which has no default value, so a quote is made with its rate given. A hosted checkout's URL is a std::string (a payload of unbounded length), and so is the client secret inside its secret. Mandates (BECS, SEPA debit) and subscriptions are not in the contract.

Status. New in 0.23.0. Experimental until two real implementations pass the kit.
