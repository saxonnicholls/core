Detailed Description

A fact's dates, the same on every fact a ledger books (payments, on-ramp, securities): when the economic event happened, when it settles, and when we learnt of it.

The trade and the settlement are kept apart because the gap between them is where cash is short: a card payment is available days later, a counter payment when the agent's file settles, a share trade at T+2. settles_on is a calendar date in the settlement account's own calendar (its time zone is the account's, not the fact's), present once known; a provider that gives none leaves it to the ledger's intake, which estimates it with finance/business_day.hpp and marks it settles_on_estimated.

Provenance, not content: known_at (when we recorded the delivery) is never part of a fact's content: two deliveries of one fact are known at different times and are the same fact. A content comparison leaves it out.
