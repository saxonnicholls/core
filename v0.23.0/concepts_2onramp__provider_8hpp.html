Detailed Description

snicholls::onramp_provider: anything that buys a stablecoin with fiat at a venue and withdraws it to an address, with the venue's facts for a ledger.

Overview. An on-ramp turns fiat held at a venue into a stablecoin and sends it on chain: quote, buy, then withdraw to a whitelisted address, with balances at every step and statement for the ledger. Moving funds between the venue's own accounts (an exchange's trading and funding accounts) is never hidden inside withdraw: an implementation that needs it offers it as an explicit, idempotent operation of its own, and withdraw with too little in the account it draws from is insufficient_funds. This is the concept half of a pair; the pure virtual half is onramp_provider_interface (interfaces/onramp_provider.hpp), with onramp_provider_adapter<O> and onramp_provider_ref.

The contract (interfaces/onramp_provider_conformance.hpp checks it):

  • Every amount is an exact money (money/money.hpp) at its asset's scale (USDC 6, ETH 18, AUD 2); a price is an exact rate (money/rate.hpp) from the asset to the quote currency, in whole units. Never a double or a string.
  • buy is idempotent on client_order_id, withdraw on client_reference (each at most 64 characters, client_id): the same id and request return the same order or withdrawal; a different request is conflict.
  • A buy with max_price never fills above it (rejected when the venue is above it); more than the balance is insufficient_funds.
  • A withdrawal to a chain the asset does not support, or to a malformed address, is invalid_argument; more than the available balance (the amount plus the network fee) is insufficient_funds.
  • An unknown order or withdrawal id is not_found.
  • statement(query) pages the venue's facts for the ledger (onramp_fact, booked once by fact_id): one per fill or per withdrawal sent, newest page first, oldest first within a page. A fact is never rounded: it carries the venue's stated amounts in the venue's own assets at the venue's precision, apart from the chain asset. A withdrawal fact is the 1:1 move from the venue asset to the chain asset; an amount the chain asset cannot hold exactly is an error, never a rounding. Every fact passes validate.
  • Errors follow the redaction rule of capability_error.hpp.
auto venue = make_venue();
const auto filled = buy_usdc(venue, "c-1", *parse_money(aud, "153")); // 100 USDC, exactly
const auto facts = venue.statement({onramp_statement_source::fills, {}, 100});

Relationships. money/money.hpp, money/rate.hpp, capability_error.hpp, capability_ids.hpp, time/utc.hpp; the twin interfaces/onramp_provider.hpp, the reference interfaces/onramp_provider_reference.hpp, the kit interfaces/onramp_provider_conformance.hpp. Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations. memory_onramp_provider (interfaces/onramp_provider_reference.hpp, for tests). Above core: an OKX venue.

Guarantees. is_evm_address and same_value are constexpr and noexcept; validate is constexpr and noexcept. Thread safety is the implementation's.

Errors. capability_error, as the contract says; validate answers invalid_argument with a fixed message.

Performance. Not benchmarked: a venue is I/O bound.

Tests and demos. tests/concepts/onramp_provider_test.cpp, demos/concepts/onramp_provider_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <concepts>, <cstddef>, <cstdint>, <optional>, <string_view>, <vector>, capability_error.hpp, capability_ids.hpp, money/money.hpp, money/rate.hpp, time/utc.hpp, uint256.hpp.

Known issues. onramp_quote holds a rate, which has no default value, so the quote is made with all its members (an aggregate with .price given). Selling (an off-ramp) and fiat withdrawal from the venue are not in the contract.

Status. New in 0.23.0. Experimental until two real implementations pass the kit.
