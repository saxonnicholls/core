Detailed Description

snicholls::ml knowledge distillation: the forward and reverse KL between a teacher's and a student's distributions at a temperature, fused and stable, with the student's gradient; and the variant that keeps only the teacher's top-k logits.

Overview. Distillation trains a student language model to match a teacher's next-token distribution at every response position (TRAINING.md T13). With temperature T, p = softmax(teacher / T) and q = softmax(student / T) (Hinton et al. 2015, eq. 1):

  • forward_kl{T}: KL(p || q) = Σ p (log p - log q), mean-seeking, Hinton's soft-target cross-entropy less the teacher's entropy (the same gradient). ∂/∂student_i = (q_i - p_i) / T (Hinton's eq. 2).
  • reverse_kl{T}: KL(q || p) = Σ q (log q - log p), mode-seeking, as on-policy distillation (MiniLLM, GKD) uses. ∂/∂student_i = q_i (log q_i - log p_i - KL) / T.

Hinton multiplies the soft loss by T² so its gradient keeps its size as T changes; here that factor is the caller's scale. Every log-probability is (x_i - max) · (1/T) - log Σ e^((x - max)/T), never a logarithm of a probability, so the loss is finite whatever the logits; a zero probability contributes nothing.

  • distill_loss(params, student, teacher, dstudent, scale): one row, the loss returned and the gradient written (dstudent may be student).
  • distill_rows(backend, params, students, teachers, mask, losses, dstudents, scale): every row of a batch on the backend (TRAINING.md T9), the masked rows (the prompt) 0 in loss and gradient.
  • teacher_topk(logits, ids, values), distill_loss_topk and distill_topk_rows: the forward KL against the teacher's k largest logits only, renormalised over them, p̃ = softmax(values / T): Σ_j p̃_j (log p̃_j - log q_{id_j}), gradient (q - p̃) / T with p̃ 0 off the top-k. A teacher's logits stored this way take k numbers and k indices a position instead of the vocabulary. It is an approximation (the tail's mass is dropped), exact when the teacher puts no mass outside its top-k.
// Forward KL(teacher || student) at T = 2 over the response positions, the gradient of the mean times T^2
const ml::forward_kl kd{.temperature = 2.0};
std::array<double, rows> losses{};
std::array<double, rows * vocab> grad{};
ml::distill_rows<double>(backend, kd, student, teacher, mask, losses, grad, 4.0 / 2.0);

Relationships. ml/softmax.hpp (the same shifted sums), ml/logprobs.hpp (the mask's layout), ml/lm_head.hpp (the logits). Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations. One each, on the host; the backend-first functions are the seam for a device.

Guarantees. constexpr, no allocation, noexcept for the standard floating-point types. Two passes over each row and the teacher's, three with the gradient; teacher_topk is O(V · k).

Errors. Preconditions, asserted: temperature > 0; the rows' sizes agree; k at most V; top-k indices below V.

Definitions. The softened softmax, the gradient (q - p) / T and the T² factor: checked against Hinton et al. 2015 (arXiv 1503.02531, eqs. 1-2, §2). The reverse KL and its gradient are derived here and checked by central differences; the top-k renormalisation is the usual sparse-logit form, recalled.

Performance. distill_rows with the forward KL against the same loops by hand, after a bit-for-bit check (benchmarks/ml/distill_benchmark.cpp), 16 rows of 50,304 float logits: 31.1 ms against 30.1 ms, level (Xeon W-3245, -O2, Apple clang 17, 2026-11-19, one thread, CPU time, load average about 100: a record, not a quiet-machine figure).

Tests and demos. tests/ml/distill_test.cpp, demos/ml/distill_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <cassert>, <concepts>, <cstddef>, <cstdint>, <limits>, <span>, <type_traits>, snicholls/core/math/constexpr_math.hpp, ml/row.hpp.

Known issues. A mix of forward and reverse (the generalised JSD of GKD) is two calls and a weighted sum.

Status. Experimental, new in 0.16.0.
