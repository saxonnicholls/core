Detailed Description

snicholls::security_fact and snicholls::security_provider: what a broker, custodian or registry tells a ledger about securities (trades and their settlement, corporate actions, repos, fund dealing), and the capability that reports it.

Overview. One ledger books crypto, fiat with its FX, and securities. The securities side reaches it as security_facts, a sibling of payment_fact (concepts/payment_provider.hpp) with the same identity (source, provider_account, event_id, fact_id, booked once by fact_id) and the same dates (fact_dates), plus what a security needs:

Field What
kind security_fact_kind: trades and settlement, entitlements and distributions, reorganisations, rights, takeovers, repos, accruals and collateral, and fund dealing (application, redemption, switch, distribution reinvested)
instrument, isin the security as an asset, <SYMBOL>:<VENUE> at its scale (BHP:ASX at 0, a unit trust at 4), and optionally its ISIN, checked by ids/check_digits.hpp
quantity the signed, exact change in the holding, in instrument
other_instrument, other_quantity, cost_apportionment the second security of a two-instrument action (a demerger's new shares, a scrip offer's bidder, a switch's target fund, a right's underlying) and the share of the cost base it takes
new_for_old the ratio of a split, consolidation, bonus or rights issue (3-for-2 is 3/2)
price, consideration, fees, taxes money: the price of one unit, the gross cash (signed: negative paid, positive received), fees, and taxes withheld or charged, with franking credits as a tax attribute
dates, ex_date, record_date, pay_date fact_dates (trade, settlement, known), and a corporate action's dates as std::chrono::sys_days
repo a repo's counterparty, agreement, exact annual rate, finance::day_count_basis and maturity
party, refs our customer's uuid (never personal data) and the broker's or registry's ids

validate(security_fact) is the check every fact passes before it leaves a provider; cash_movement is the cash it moves (consideration less fees and taxes). The ledger owns the cost work (parcels, restatement, cost-base reduction, rollover); core owns the contract only.

The capability. security_provider is the broker, custodian or registry side, with the shape of payment_provider so servers' webhook inbox, worker and outbox serve both: verify_webhook authenticates a delivery without I/O (fails closed), the worker calls resolve(event) for the authoritative facts, the reconcilers read statement(query) page by page, and holdings(as_of) gives the positions the provider holds for us at the end of a day, by trade date and by settlement, for the break check. Its webhook types (webhook_delivery, webhook_event, owned_delivery) are payment_provider's; an event about a security is event_kind::security_updated. This is the concept half of a pair; the pure virtual half is security_provider_interface (interfaces/security_provider.hpp), with security_provider_adapter<P> and security_provider_ref.

The contract (interfaces/security_provider_conformance.hpp checks it):

  • Every fact passes validate. Quantities, prices and cash are exact money; ratios are exact finance::ratio.
  • Amounts and positions never come from the webhook body: resolve reads them from the provider's records. An event that books nothing (event_kind::other, or a test delivery) resolves to no facts.
  • A trade is booked on its trade date (buy, sell, with dates.settles_on) and settles by a later settle fact carrying the same quantity, or fails by settle_failed. A fact with dates.settled_at moves the settled holding on that day; settle is how a trade booked unsettled settles. holdings folds exactly that.
  • Every statement line is exactly one fact, oldest first within a page, with fact_id the provider's line id.
  • Errors follow the redaction rule of capability_error.hpp.
  • Personal data stays out: a holder identification number, a name or an address is never in a fact. party is our customer's uuid.
security_fact trade;
trade.source = "broker";
trade.fact_id = "fill-20261126-1";
trade.kind = security_fact_kind::buy;
trade.instrument = bhp; // BHP:ASX, whole shares
trade.isin = isin_text{"AU000000BHP4"}; // checked by validate
trade.quantity = money::from_units(bhp, 100);
trade.price = *parse_money(checked_asset("AUD", 3), "45.125");
trade.consideration = -*parse_money(aud, "4512.50"); // paid
trade.fees = *parse_money(aud, "9.95");
trade.dates.trade_at = now;
trade.dates.known_at = now;
trade.dates.settles_on = now.days() + std::chrono::days{2};
const bool bookable = validate(trade).is_ok();
const auto cash = cash_movement(trade); // -4522.45
// Any provider, by concept: the reconciler reads every fact the provider has.
template <security_provider P>
std::size_t count_facts(P& provider)
{
std::size_t n = 0;
std::string cursor;
for (;;) {
const auto page = provider.statement({cursor, std::nullopt, 100});
if (!page)
return n;
n += page->facts.size();
if (page->next_cursor.empty())
return n;
cursor = std::string(page->next_cursor.view());
}
}

Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations. memory_security_provider (interfaces/security_provider_reference.hpp, for tests). Above core: brokers, custodians and registries, in servers.

Guarantees. validate, cash_movement, is_instrument_code and the kind predicates are constexpr and noexcept. Thread safety is the implementation's.

Errors. capability_error; validate returns invalid_argument with a fixed message naming the rule broken.

Performance. Not benchmarked: validate is a few comparisons per field.

Tests and demos. tests/concepts/security_provider_test.cpp, demos/concepts/security_provider_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <chrono>, <concepts>, <cstddef>, <cstdint>, <optional>, <string_view>, <vector>, capability_error.hpp, capability_ids.hpp, concepts/payment_provider.hpp, containers/inplace_string.hpp, finance/day_count.hpp, finance/ratio.hpp, ids/check_digits.hpp, money/money.hpp, uuid.hpp.

Known issues. The instrument code is <SYMBOL>:<VENUE>, as core's chain tokens (USDC:8453) and the securities library's asset_code (BHP:ASX) are. price is per whole unit in the cash currency's code, at any scale at least as fine as the currency's (a share priced at 0.5 cents is AUD at scale 3). Holdings are positions, not cost: cost is the ledger's.

Status. New in 0.27.0. Experimental until two real implementations pass the kit.
