template<math::math_number T, std::size_t Positions, std::size_t HeadDim>

struct snicholls::ml::rope_mode_table< T, Positions, HeadDim >

Many tokens' cosines and sines in a ggml mode: a rope_source by token index, built by a constexpr constructor (in a constexpr variable for a fixed grid, at start-up for a long one).
