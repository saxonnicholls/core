ml Directory Reference

Files

 		activations.hpp
 snicholls::ml activations: sigmoid, softplus, logit, ReLU, ReLU², SiLU, GELU (exact and tanh) and the logit soft cap, each with its derivative, all constexpr.
 		attention.hpp
 snicholls::ml attention for one query: attend over a block of keys and values in one pass, its backward pass, attention_accumulator, the partial that merges across workers, and attention_window for causal and sliding-window masks.
 		bpe.hpp
 snicholls::ml::bpe_encode_piece, bpe_encode_long_piece and bpe_decode: byte-level BPE, token for token what tiktoken gives, with the vocabulary as the caller's lookup and no allocation.
 		embedding.hpp
 snicholls::ml embeddings: a token's row of a table, GPT-2's token plus position, and the backward pass that adds a gradient into the token's row.
 		minifloat.hpp
 snicholls::ml::minifloat<E, M, Kind>: the low-precision floating-point formats of machine learning (bfloat16, float16, FP8 E4M3 and E5M2, the MX FP6 and FP4) as one template, with exact decoding, round-to-nearest-even and stochastic rounding, all constexpr.
 		norm.hpp
 snicholls::ml normalisation: layer_norm and rms_norm over one row, forward and backward, with or without learned parameters.
 		optim.hpp
 snicholls::ml training arithmetic: the AdamW step, learning-rate schedules, clipping by the global norm, Muon's Newton-Schulz polynomial, and the FLOPs and utilisation of a transformer.
 		quantize.hpp
 snicholls::ml quantised weights: ggml's Q8_0 and Q4_0 blocks and the OCP MXFP4 block, byte for byte as ggml lays them out, with quantisation, dequantisation and the integer dot products, plus symmetric int8 rows.
 		rope.hpp
 snicholls::ml rotary position embedding: the frequencies, a table of cosines and sines the compiler can build, and the rotation of a head, forward and inverse.
 		row.hpp
 snicholls::ml::row and mutable_row: what the ml functions accept as a row of numbers, and the spans they work on.
 		sampling.hpp
 snicholls::ml sampling the next token: argmax, sampling from probabilities or from logits at a temperature, top_k and top-k sampling with a fixed-size buffer, and nucleus (top-p) sampling with the caller's scratch.
 		softmax.hpp
 snicholls::ml softmax and losses: softmax, log_softmax, logsumexp, cross-entropy with its fused gradient, bits per byte, and online_softmax, a running softmax that merges across workers.
