functional Directory Reference

Files

 		bind.hpp
 snicholls::fn::bind with placeholders arg<N> / _1 ... _9: arguments bound in any position, constexpr.
 		combinators.hpp
 snicholls::fn combinators: functions that take functions and return functions, all constexpr.
 		curry.hpp
 snicholls::fn::curry, uncurry, partial and partial_right: a function of many arguments as a chain of functions of fewer, all constexpr.
 		fold.hpp
 snicholls::fn::foldl, foldr, scan, foldl_of, foldr_of, unfold and iterate_n: folds over parameter packs and sequences, and unfolds into arrays, all constexpr.
 		instances.hpp
 The type-class instances of functional/typeclasses.hpp for std::optional (Maybe), snicholls::result (Either), std::tuple and std::pair (products), std::array (fixed lists), std::basic_string, std::vector and containers::inplace_vector (what functional/list.hpp's filter and partition return), all constexpr.
 		lazy.hpp
 Lazy evaluation, constexpr: lazy (a thunk evaluated at most once) and infinite streams with iterate, repeat, cycle, unfold, map, filter, zip_with, scanl, drop, drop_while, take and take_while, finished into std::arrays at compile time.
 		list.hpp
 Haskell's Data.List and OCaml's List over std::array (lists whose length is known at compile time) and, for the folds and searches, any input range; Foldable's length, null, elem, sum, product, maximum, minimum, fold_map; and fst, snd, map_tuple. All constexpr.
 		match.hpp
 snicholls::fn::match: pattern matching over values, std::variant and snicholls::result, with guards, exhaustiveness checked at compile time, and destructuring of tuples and plain aggregates.
 		monad.hpp
 Maybe and Either helpers over std::optional and snicholls::result: lift, sequence / traverse (Traversable), fold_m, zip_with_m, replicate_m, for_m, when_m / unless_m, the eliminators maybe and either, and value_or, from_maybe, map_maybe, cat_maybes. All constexpr.
 		ocaml.hpp
 OCaml's standard modules over core's types: fn::ocaml::option (Option), fn::ocaml::result (Result) and fn::ocaml::list (List), with OCaml's names and argument orders; and the idioms for |>, @@, labelled and optional arguments and functors. All constexpr.
 		pointwise.hpp
 snicholls::fn::pointwise{f}: a function that is a value of an algebra, added, scaled, compared and combined with && / || point by point. All constexpr.
 		recursion.hpp
 Recursion schemes over list-shaped data, cata, ana, hylo and para, and a trampoline for deep recursion without the stack, all constexpr.
 		rewrite.hpp
 Rewriting by rules, Wolfram's /. and //. over values: rule, rules, except, replace_once, replace_repeated, and over any tree transform_up, transform_down and rewrite_up (Uniplate's transform and rewrite). All constexpr.
 		rust.hpp
 Rust's Option methods over std::optional (fn::rust::option), std::cmp's ordering helpers (fn::rust::cmp) and Into (fn::rust::into<T>), each the one Rust has and C++20 lacks. All constexpr.
 		rust_iter.hpp
 Rust's iterator and slice methods that C++20 and core lack: windows, chunks, chunks_exact, dedup (fn::rust::slice), and enumerate, step_by, find_map, max_by_key, min_by_key, max_by, min_by, nth, last, try_for_each (fn::rust::iter). All constexpr and allocation-free.
 		state.hpp
 The Identity, Const, Reader, Writer and State monads as constexpr value types, with their Functor, Applicative and Monad instances.
 		tabulate.hpp
 snicholls::fn::tabulate<N>(f): a function of an index, memoised at compile time into a table lookup.
 		typeclasses.hpp
 Haskell's type classes as C++20 concepts with instance traits: Semigroup, Monoid, Functor, Applicative, Monad, Alternative, Bifunctor and Profunctor, and the functions over them, all constexpr.
 		types.hpp
 Type-level functional programming under functional's names: fn::types::map_t, filter_t, foldl_t, foldr_t, zip_t, zip_with_t and friends, aliases of snicholls::meta::type_list's algorithms.
