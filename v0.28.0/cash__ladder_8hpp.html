Detailed Description

snicholls::finance::make_cash_ladder: an opening balance and dated cash flows as a ladder of per-date inflow, outflow, net and cumulative cash, the first date below a threshold, and an overdue bucket.

Overview. A liquidity forecast: a provider that pays twice a week, stock-market settlements in flight and card payouts due all move one cash account, and the question is whether the account stays above its floor on every date ahead. make_cash_ladder(flows, terms, rungs) takes

  • the flows, each a value date (std::chrono::sys_days) and a signed money (positive in, negative out), in any order;
  • the terms: today (as_of), the opening balance, the threshold and the overdue policy;
  • the caller's span of rungs, which it fills: one rung per date that has a flow, in date order, each with that date's inflow, outflow, net and the cumulative position at its close.

It returns the ladder: the filled rungs, the position at the start, the closing and the lowest position, the first date the position is below the threshold, and the overdue bucket. A flow dated before as_of is overdue; what happens to it is the caller's policy, set separately for inflows and outflows (overdue_policy):

  • overdue_treatment::count: trusted to have happened, counted in the starting position;
  • overdue_treatment::report_only: in the overdue bucket only, not in any position;
  • overdue_treatment::due_today: moved to the as_of rung, as though it falls due today.

Every overdue flow is in the bucket whatever its treatment. The default overdue_policy{} is the prudent one, overdue_policy::prudent(): overdue inflows reported but not counted (money late in is not money in), overdue outflows due today (money late out is owed now).

const sys_days today = 2026y / November / 26;
const std::array<cash_flow, 6> flows{{
{today - days{2}, a("1200.00")}, // a provider payout that has not arrived: overdue
{today - days{1}, a("-300.00")}, // a share purchase that has not settled yet: overdue
{today, a("-450.00")}, // card payouts today
{today + days{1}, a("-900.00")}, // a stock-market settlement
{today + days{4}, a("2500.00")}, // Australia Post's twice-weekly settlement
{today + days{1}, a("150.00")}, // a refund received
}};
std::array<cash_rung, flows.size() + 1> rungs{};
const cash_ladder_terms terms{today, a("1000.00"), a("0.00"), overdue_policy::prudent()};
const auto ladder = make_cash_ladder(flows, terms, rungs);
// ladder->rungs: per date inflow, outflow, net, cumulative; ladder->first_below: the first breach

Use it for a forecast over flows already known; it does not model, estimate or net across accounts. Not for more than one asset: a ladder is one account's currency, and a flow, an opening or a threshold of another asset is money_errc::asset_mismatch (convert first, with money/rate.hpp, and say at which rate).

Relationships. money/money.hpp (money, add, compare, money_errc); a ledger's value-dated pending settlements feed it, and its buffers and breaks (a liquidity shortfall) are the ledger's. finance/business_day.hpp moves a value date to a business day before it reaches the ladder. Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations / variants. One function; the overdue policy is its variation.

Guarantees.

  • Exact: every sum is money arithmetic over uint256 minor units; nothing is rounded or converted.
  • constexpr, noexcept, and no allocation: the rungs are written into the caller's span, and the returned ladder's rungs is the filled prefix of it. flows.size() + 1 rungs are always enough.
  • Rungs are in strictly increasing date order, every one on or after as_of. Each rung's inflow and outflow are not negative, net == inflow - outflow, and cumulative is the start plus every net up to and including it.
  • first_below is as_of when the starting position is already below the threshold, else the first rung whose cumulative is below it, else empty. "Below" is strict: a position equal to the threshold is not a breach.
  • On an error the span's contents are unspecified and nothing else is changed.
  • O(n log k + n k) for n flows on k dates in the worst case (an insertion shifts later rungs), O(n log k) when the flows come in date order.

Errors. money_errc:

  • asset_mismatch: a flow, the threshold or the opening is of another asset;
  • overflow: a sum exceeds 2^256 - 1 minor units;
  • buffer_too_small: the flows fall on more dates than the span has rungs.

Performance. Not benchmarked: one pass of money additions per flow and one per rung.

Tests and demos. tests/finance/cash_ladder_test.cpp (no flows, every flow overdue under each treatment, crossing the threshold, exact arithmetic past 64 bits, unsorted input, each error, at compile time), demos/finance/cash_ladder_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <chrono>, <compare>, <cstddef>, <cstdint>, <optional>, <span>, money/money.hpp, result.hpp.

Known issues. A zero-amount flow still makes a rung for its date (an inflow of zero). Dates are value dates as given: a flow on a weekend stays on the weekend unless the caller adjusts it.

Status. New in 0.27.0. Experimental.
