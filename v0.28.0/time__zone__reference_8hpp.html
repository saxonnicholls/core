Detailed Description

snicholls::fixed_offset_zone and snicholls::two_rule_zone: the standard-library reference time_zones.

Overview. Two zones with no database behind them, for tests, examples and the kit:

  • fixed_offset_zone: one offset forever (UTC, UTC+10, an exchange that never changes its clocks);
  • two_rule_zone: a toy daylight-saving zone with one rule into daylight time and one out of it, each "the nth (or last) weekday of a month at a wall-clock time", in either hemisphere. Real zones change their rules over the years; this one never does, which is what makes it a fixed, readable test zone (and never a substitute for the IANA database).
constexpr fixed_offset_zone india{"India-like", minutes{330}, "IST"};
constexpr two_rule_zone sydney = sydney_like_zone();
constexpr zone_info summer = sydney.info(sys_days{year{2026} / January / 1});
constexpr local_info fold = sydney.info(local_days{year{2026} / April / 5} + hours{2} + minutes{30});

Relationships. Model time_zone (concepts/time_zone.hpp); pass interfaces/time_zone_conformance.hpp directly and through interfaces/time_zone.hpp. Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations / variants. The two above. sydney_like_zone() and new_york_like_zone() are ready-made two_rule_zones with today's rules of those cities (not their history).

Guarantees. Immutable values, constexpr, no allocation; safe to share between threads.

Errors. None.

Performance. Not benchmarked: six transitions are computed per info call.

Tests and demos. tests/interfaces/time_zone_reference_test.cpp, demos/interfaces/time_zone_reference_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <algorithm>, <array>, <chrono>, <string_view>, interfaces/time_zone.hpp.

Known issues. Years outside std::chrono::year's range (about +-32767) are not supported by two_rule_zone.

Status. New in 0.27.0.
