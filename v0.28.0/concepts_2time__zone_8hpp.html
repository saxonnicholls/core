Detailed Description

snicholls::time_zone: the rules of a time zone, and conversions between UTC and wall-clock time.

Overview. An instant (utc<D>) has no zone. A zone turns instants into wall-clock times and back: exchange sessions, cut-offs, statements, display. The data (the IANA database) is run-time data and the operating system's or a library's; core holds the contract and the conversions written over it, so every zone plugs in:

  • z.name(): the zone's name ("Australia/Sydney", "UTC+10");
  • z.info(t) at an instant std::chrono::sys_seconds: the zone_info in force (offset, daylight saving, abbreviation, and the interval [begin, end) the rules hold for);
  • z.info(l) at a wall-clock time std::chrono::local_seconds: a local_info saying whether it is unique, nonexistent (in a gap: clocks jumped forward) or ambiguous (in a fold: clocks went back), with the rules on either side.

Written over the concept: offset_at(z, t), to_local(z, t), and from_local(z, l, options), which returns the instant or time_zone_errc::nonexistent_local_time / ambiguous_local_time, never a silent guess: gaps and folds are resolved only by a policy the caller names (local_options). to_text(t, offset) writes RFC 3339 with a numeric offset.

constexpr two_rule_zone new_york = new_york_like_zone();
const auto close = cut_off(new_york, year{2026} / July / 1); // 16:00 EDT
const local_seconds in_gap = local_days{year{2026} / March / 8} + hours{2} + minutes{30};
const auto refused = from_local(new_york, in_gap); // rejected by default
const auto moved = from_local(new_york, in_gap, {fold_policy::reject, gap_policy::next_valid});

The contract (interfaces/time_zone_conformance.hpp checks it):

  • info(t) holds at t: begin <= t < end, and the interval after end begins at end;
  • a wall-clock time is unique exactly when one instant has it, and then from_local(to_local(t)) == t;
  • in a gap first holds before the transition and second from it; in a fold first is the earlier offset's;
  • an offset is less than a day either way.

Relationships. time/utc.hpp, time/rfc3339.hpp; the twin interfaces/time_zone.hpp, the references interfaces/time_zone_reference.hpp (fixed_offset_zone, two_rule_zone), the kit interfaces/time_zone_conformance.hpp. Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations. fixed_offset_zone and two_rule_zone (a toy daylight-saving zone for tests). Above core: the IANA database adapters of ts-moveables and crtp (utc_tz).

Guarantees. Every function here is constexpr when the zone's info is; none allocates. from_local is exact: one or two calls to info.

Errors. from_local: nonexistent_local_time and ambiguous_local_time under the reject policies (the default). to_text: std::errc::not_supported in the text's ec for an offset with seconds (before about 1900), std::errc::value_too_large when the local year leaves 0000 to 9999.

Performance. Not benchmarked: the cost is the zone's info.

Tests and demos. tests/concepts/time_zone_test.cpp, demos/concepts/time_zone_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <chrono>, <concepts>, <cstdint>, <string_view>, <type_traits>, containers/inplace_string.hpp, result.hpp, time/rfc3339.hpp, time/utc.hpp.

Known issues. Leap seconds do not exist in utc, so none appear in local time. RFC 3339 cannot carry an offset with seconds.

Status. New in 0.27.0. Experimental until two real implementations pass the kit.
