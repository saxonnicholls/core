Detailed Description

snicholls::role_store: which party holds which role in which tenant, append-only, with its history.

Overview. Authorisation re-reads a caller's roles from here on every request, so a revocation takes effect on the next call. A grant names a party (its uuid, the subject of token_claims), a tenant (an organisation, or platform_tenant for staff), a role, who granted it, why, when, and optionally until when (an auditor's window). Nothing is updated in place: a grant or a revoke appends an event, and the current view is derived. The store records and does not decide: what a role permits (its permissions), rank rules, the single owner and bootstrap admins are the policy's (servers' role catalogue, routes/rbac.hpp). This is the concept half of a pair; the pure virtual half is role_store_interface (interfaces/role_store.hpp), with role_store_adapter<S> and role_store_ref.

The contract (interfaces/role_store_conformance.hpp checks it):

  • grants_of(party, tenant, now) is the party's active grants in that tenant only (a staff role in platform_tenant never shows in a customer's tenant); a grant with expires_at <= now is not active;
  • members(tenant, now) is every active grant in the tenant;
  • grant of an existing (party, tenant, role) replaces it (a new expiry, a new reason) and appends an event;
  • revoke of a grant that is not there is capability_errc::not_found and appends nothing;
  • history(tenant) is every event of the tenant in order, oldest first, with the actor, the reason and any confirmation;
  • a confirmation or reason longer than short_text is invalid_argument, never truncated;
  • a failure of the store behind it is returned (unavailable, internal), never an empty answer, and follows the redaction rule of capability_error.hpp.
// Re-read on every request: does the party hold `role` in `tenant` now? A store that cannot answer denies.
template <role_store S>
bool holds(const S& roles, const uuid& party, std::string_view tenant, std::string_view role, utc_s now)
{
const auto grants = roles.grants_of(party, tenant, now);
if (grants.is_err())
return false;
for (const auto& g : *grants)
if (g.role == role)
return true;
return false;
}

Relationships. uuid.hpp (the party), capability_ids.hpp (tenant_id, role_id, short_text), time/utc.hpp; concepts/token_verifier.hpp (whose subject is the party); the twin interfaces/role_store.hpp, the reference interfaces/role_store_reference.hpp, the kit interfaces/role_store_conformance.hpp. Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations.

  • kv_role_store<S> (interfaces/role_store_reference.hpp) over any key_value_store, and memory_role_store, it over core's reference_key_value_store: standard library only.
  • Above core: servers' Postgres store, an events table and a materialised current view.

Guarantees. role_grant and role_event hold no std::string: both are trivially copyable. Thread safety is the implementation's.

Errors. capability_error, as the contract says.

Performance. Not benchmarked: reads are a prefix scan of one party's grants.

Tests and demos. tests/concepts/role_store_test.cpp, demos/concepts/role_store_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <concepts>, <optional>, <string_view>, <vector>, capability_error.hpp, capability_ids.hpp, uuid.hpp.

Known issues. A role id names a role across tenants; a product that needs per-tenant role definitions keeps them in its policy.

Status. New in 0.27.0, lifted from servers. Experimental until two real implementations pass the kit.
