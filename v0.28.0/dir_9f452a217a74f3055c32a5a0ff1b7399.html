ml Directory Reference

Files

 		activations.hpp
 snicholls::ml activations: sigmoid, softplus, logit, ReLU, ReLU², SiLU, GELU (exact, tanh and quick), ELU, leaky ReLU, hard sigmoid, hard swish, xIELU, step, sign and the logit soft cap, each with its derivative, all constexpr.
 		alibi.hpp
 snicholls::ml ALiBi: each head's slope as ggml computes it, a compile-time table of slopes, the linear bias of a query and a key, and attention with that bias, forward and backward.
 		attention.hpp
 snicholls::ml attention for one query: attend over a block of keys and values in one pass, its backward pass, attention_accumulator, the partial that merges across workers, attention_window for causal and sliding-window masks, and attention_keys for every attention_mask: causal, bidirectional and block-causal, with key padding.
 		attention_layer.hpp
 snicholls::ml::attention_forward, attention_forward_sequence and attention_backward: a transformer's attention for one token at one position, or over a sequence for training, and its backward pass: the Q, K and V projections, QK norm, RoPE, the KV cache, grouped-query attention, the soft cap and the output projection, as the configuration says.
 		attention_sink.hpp
 snicholls::ml::attend_with_sink and its backward pass: attention for one query with a learnable sink logit, a virtual key every query attends to that carries no value (gpt-oss), with keys and values of different widths.
 		bitnet.hpp
 snicholls::ml BitNet b1.58: absmean ternary weights, per-token absmax activations, the straight-through estimator for quantisation-aware training, the ternary x int8 dot product and BitLinear, all constexpr.
 		bitnet_kernels.hpp
 snicholls::ml BitNet's packed ternary layouts: I2_S (2 bits a weight, multiply-add kernels) and the lookup-table formats TL1 (pairs of weights, 4-bit indices) and TL2 (triples, 4-bit magnitude and a sign bit), byte for byte as bitnet.cpp writes them, with reference kernels, all constexpr.
 		bpe.hpp
 snicholls::ml::bpe_encode_piece, bpe_encode_long_piece and bpe_decode: byte-level BPE, token for token what tiktoken gives, with the vocabulary as the caller's lookup and no allocation.
 		bpe_merges.hpp
 snicholls::ml::merge_encode: byte-pair encoding driven by a priority over pairs (Hugging Face's and GGUF's merge lists, or SentencePiece's scores), with byte fallback; the merge list as a sorted index; and GPT-2's byte-to-unicode map, built at compile time.
 		collapse.hpp
 snicholls::ml collapse safeguards for joint-embedding training: embedding_moments (mergeable per-dimension standard deviation, covariance and effective rank), VICReg's variance and covariance terms, and LeJEPA's SIGReg (sketched Epps-Pulley), each with its gradient.
 		composed_block.hpp
 snicholls::ml transformer blocks composed from an attention part and an MLP part, either kind of each: x += attention(norm(x)); x += mlp(norm(x)), batched for training (returning the MLP's auxiliary losses) and token by token for decoding; DeepSeek-V3's block, multi-head latent attention with a mixture of experts, as latent_moe_block_* with its buffers' sizes and layouts.
 		control_vector.hpp
 snicholls::ml control vectors: a direction per layer added to the residual stream after layers il_start to il_end, in llama.cpp's layout, with the sum of several at given strengths and the backward pass for training one.
 		conv.hpp
 snicholls::ml convolutions of rank 1, 2 and 3: im2col / col2im, the convolution forward and backward (direct and through im2col), groups and depthwise, and the transposed convolution, all constexpr over the caller's spans.
 		delta_rule.hpp
 snicholls::ml the gated delta rule (Gated DeltaNet, Qwen3-Next's and Kimi Linear's linear attention): the recurrent form, the chunked form through the WY / UT representation and a triangular solve, the backward pass, and one token at a time for decoding.
 		diloco.hpp
 snicholls::ml arithmetic of data-parallel training across machines: the fixed-order sum of the workers' parts, DiLoCo's pseudo-gradient, its bfloat16 compression with error feedback, and its outer step.
 		distill.hpp
 snicholls::ml knowledge distillation: the forward and reverse KL between a teacher's and a student's distributions at a temperature, fused and stable, with the student's gradient; and the variant that keeps only the teacher's top-k logits.
 		embedding.hpp
 snicholls::ml embeddings: a token's row of a table, GPT-2's token plus position, and the backward pass that adds a gradient into the token's row.
 		expert_parallel.hpp
 snicholls::ml expert parallelism, the arithmetic: which rank holds which experts, the dispatch and combine layouts, and a reference all-to-all over in-process ranks that fixes the order of every received row.
 		gather.hpp

 		gla.hpp
 snicholls::ml gated linear attention (GLA): the recurrent form, the chunked form, the backward pass, and one token at a time for decoding.
 		glu.hpp
 snicholls::ml gated linear units: ReGLU, GEGLU (tanh, erf and quick), SwiGLU, gpt-oss's clamped SwiGLU and SwiGLU with a clamp, for one element and over rows, forward and backward, all constexpr.
 		grammar.hpp
 snicholls::ml grammar-constrained decoding: a GBNF compiler (at run time from a string, at compile time from a fixed_string into fixed-size tables), the pushdown matcher and the token mask over a vocabulary.
 		hadamard.hpp
 snicholls::ml Hadamard rotations: the fast Walsh-Hadamard transform, the orthonormal and block forms ggml computes bit for bit, the randomised transform of QuaRot and SpinQuant, and folding a rotation into weights before quantisation, all constexpr.
 		jepa.hpp
 snicholls::ml joint embedding predictive architectures: the predictor's configuration, sizes, input and output, the normalised targets, the prediction losses (L2, L1, smooth L1, cosine) with their gradients, and the target encoder as an exponential moving average with its momentum schedule.
 		json_schema.hpp

 		kv_cache.hpp

 		kv_cache_quant.hpp

 		kv_cells.hpp
 snicholls::ml::kv_cells: which position and which sequences each slot of a KV cache holds, llama.cpp's sequence operations on them (remove a range, copy, keep, add to and divide positions), the K-shift that re-rotates cached keys after a position change, and attention over the cells a query sees.
 		latent_attention.hpp
 snicholls::ml multi-head latent attention (MLA, DeepSeek-V2 and V3): queries, keys and values from low-rank latents with decoupled RoPE; the training passes over sequences through a linalg backend; a KV cache holding only the latent and the rotary key; and the absorbed form for decoding.
 		latent_block.hpp
 snicholls::ml transformer blocks whose attention is multi-head latent attention: x += mla(norm(x)); x += mlp(norm(x)), for decoding with a latent cache and, for training, over a batch of sequences with its backward pass.
 		linear.hpp
 snicholls::ml::linear: y = W x + b over any weight storage: dense rows of float, double or bfloat16, or ggml's quantised blocks.
 		lm_head.hpp

 		logprobs.hpp
 snicholls::ml log-probabilities of given tokens: per token from logits (fused and stable), with the entropy, summed or averaged over a sequence's response tokens, and their backward passes.
 		lora.hpp

 		masking.hpp

 		mixer_layer.hpp

 		mlp.hpp
