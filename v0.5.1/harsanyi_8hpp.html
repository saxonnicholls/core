Detailed Description

Harsanyi dividends: the part of a game's value created by each coalition and by no smaller one.

Overview. Every game is a sum of "unanimity" pieces: v(S) = sum over T within S of d(T). The dividend d(T) is what the players of T create together beyond everything their smaller coalitions already create: d({i}) is player i alone, d({i, j}) the pure interaction of the pair, and so on. The Shapley value shares each dividend equally among its coalition, phi_i = sum over T containing i of d(T) / |T|, which is the cleanest way to see why it is fair. A game is additive exactly when every dividend of two or more players is zero, and the dividends of a Taylor expansion are its terms: the pair dividend of spot and volatility is the vanna term.

// P&L when spot moves (player 0), when volatility moves (player 1), and both: the 0.5 left over
// when both move is the cross term, which neither earns alone.
constexpr std::array<double, 4> pnl{0.0, 2.0, 1.0, 3.5};
constexpr auto dividends = game::harsanyi_dividends<2>(game::from_table(pnl));
static_assert(dividends[0b11] == 0.5);
// Shapley shares each dividend equally among its coalition: the cross term half each.
const auto shares = game::shapley_from_dividends(std::span<const double>(dividends));

to_dividends is the Mobius transform and from_dividends the zeta transform, each in place in O(n 2^n) additions, each the other's inverse.

Relationships. game/shapley.hpp for the same values from lifts; game/coalition.hpp for tables. Group: snicholls-core.

Guarantees. constexpr; the transforms allocate nothing and are exact for integer values.

Errors. None; a table's size must be a power of two (asserted).

Performance. Not benchmarked: n 2^(n-1) subtractions.

Tests and demos. tests/game/harsanyi_test.cpp, demos/game/harsanyi_demo.cpp.
