Detailed Description

ISO 8601 week dates: the week-based year, the week number and the weekday of a day, both ways, constexpr.

Overview. ISO 8601 numbers weeks from Monday, and week 1 of a year is the week with the year's first Thursday in it (equivalently, the week of 4 January). A year has 52 or 53 weeks, and the days of late December and early January can belong to the neighbouring week-based year: 2024-12-30 is 2025-W01-1. Settlement cycles, reporting calendars and futures expiries are written this way.

  • to_iso_week_date(day) gives the iso_week_date of a std::chrono::sys_days;
  • to_sys_days(week_date) goes back, for a valid week date;
  • make_iso_week_date(year, week, weekday) validates first, and refuses week 53 of a 52-week year;
  • iso_weeks_in_year(year) is 52 or 53.
constexpr snicholls::iso_week_date w = snicholls::to_iso_week_date(sys_days{2024y / December / 30d});
static_assert(w == snicholls::iso_week_date{2025, 1, 1}); // a Monday in week 1 of 2025
constexpr auto settlement = snicholls::make_iso_week_date(2026, 53, 4); // 2026 has 53 weeks
static_assert(settlement && snicholls::to_sys_days(*settlement) == sys_days{2026y / December / 31d});
static_assert(!snicholls::make_iso_week_date(2027, 53, 1)); // 2027 has 52

Relationships. time/civil.hpp (the day arithmetic underneath), time/names.hpp (weekday names), time/business_days.hpp, time/utc.hpp (utc<D>::days() gives the sys_days). Group: snicholls-core.

Implementations. One.

Guarantees. constexpr, noexcept, O(1), no allocation. to_sys_days(to_iso_week_date(d)) == d for every day (tested over 1600 to 2400 in full), and a week date orders as its day does.

Errors. make_iso_week_date returns week_date_errc::invalid_week or invalid_weekday.

Performance. Two days_from_civil and one civil_from_days; not benchmarked.

Tests and demos. tests/time/week_date_test.cpp, against Python's datetime.date.isocalendar() and fromisocalendar() (scripts/generate_week_date_fixtures.py, tests/fixtures/time/week_date.txt); demos/time/week_date_demo.cpp.

Dependencies. <chrono>, <compare>, <cstdint>, result.hpp, time/civil.hpp.

Known issues. The year is a std::chrono::sys_days year: within +-32767, the range of std::chrono::year.

Status. New in 0.23.0.
