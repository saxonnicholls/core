Detailed Description

template<typename Point, std::size_t K, typename Traits = kd_point_traits>
class snicholls::containers::kd_tree< Point, K, Traits >

A balanced k-d tree over the caller's points, stored as a permutation of their indices.

Template Parameters
PointThe point type.
KThe dimension, at least 1.
TraitsThe coordinate reader: Traits::coordinate(p, axis).
