signal Directory Reference

Files

 		complex.hpp
 snicholls::signal::complex<T>, a trivially copyable complex number for the transforms, usable in constant evaluation, and twiddle<T>(k, n), the root of unity e^(-2 pi i k / n) to the last bit the type allows.
 		convolve.hpp
 snicholls::signal convolution and correlation of real sequences, direct and through the FFT, in scipy's full, same and valid modes, and block filtering by overlap-add and overlap-save.
 		dct.hpp
 snicholls::signal discrete cosine and sine transforms of types II, III and IV through the FFT, with scipy's definitions and its orthonormal option, and the backward pass of the DCT-II.
 		fft.hpp
 snicholls::signal fast Fourier transforms of any length, at compile time and at run time: complex and real, forward and inverse, in one dimension, over rows and over N axes, with their backward passes.
 		fir.hpp
 snicholls::signal FIR filter design by the windowed sinc (scipy.signal.firwin): low-pass, high-pass, band-pass and band-stop, constexpr so a filter can be a compile-time table, with direct filtering and the frequency response.
 		hilbert.hpp
 snicholls::signal analytic signal and Hilbert transform through the FFT, as scipy.signal.hilbert, with the envelope and the backward pass.
 		iir.hpp
 snicholls::signal IIR filters: biquads with the Audio EQ Cookbook's coefficients, Butterworth design as second-order sections (low-, high-, band-pass, band-stop), sosfilt, and frequency responses, all constexpr.
 		mel.hpp
 snicholls::signal audio front ends: the mel scales (HTK and Slaney), mel filterbanks as librosa and torchaudio build them, mel spectrograms, log compression (decibels, Whisper's), OpenAI Whisper's log-mel spectrogram, MFCCs, and the backward passes.
 		resample.hpp
 snicholls::signal polyphase resampling by a rational factor up / down, as scipy.signal.resample_poly, with the windowed-sinc kernel built at compile time when the factor is known.
 		stft.hpp
 snicholls::signal short-time Fourier transform and its inverse, as torch.stft and torch.istft compute them, spectrograms, and their backward passes.
 		window.hpp
 snicholls::signal window functions - rectangular, Hann, Hamming, Blackman, Blackman-Harris, Kaiser, Gaussian, Tukey - periodic or symmetric, as constexpr values, filled spans and compile-time tables; and the COLA and NOLA conditions of an overlap-add.
